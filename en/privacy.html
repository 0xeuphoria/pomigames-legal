7. Your rights

  • Ask what data is processed, or have it corrected or deleted: pomigames.help@gmail.com. We reply within 10 working days.
  • Complain to your data protection authority (in Russia, Roskomnadzor).

8. Children

The game suits all ages but is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 14. If a child plays on a parent’s device, a parent should decide on statistics and personalised ads. If you believe we received a child’s data, contact us and we will delete it.

Data is sent to Yandex over encrypted connections. We do not keep player data on our own servers.
